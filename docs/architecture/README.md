# Architecture set

Index of the aiops-platform architecture documents. Each file states its own status in its header. The set describes the community edition: the Grafana stack is first-party and the default, other observability systems are reached through connector packs, every upstream is a plugin behind a definition and never the product, and the order of work leads to the first community release (`00` §7.1).

## The licence record

[`../design/licence-audit-2026-11.md`](../design/licence-audit-2026-11.md) is the licence record, cited in this set as "licence record §n". It answers owner decision C: for every upstream and content source the open-source edition builds on, whether its licence is suitable and whether any conflict forces a rebuild. It is engineering analysis, not legal advice; nothing it marks LAWYER is settled. `00-decisions.md` wins where the two differ, and the checklist that enforces the record is `08` §5. Its verdicts are the values of the upstream register, `tools/upstream-components.yaml` (`00` §3.2).

Its answer in short: no software base must be rebuilt for its licence, and HolmesGPT and GoAlert are both suitable as optional plugins. What must be first-party is content, the compliance control catalogue with its wording and mappings. Two upstream charts are replaced by templates of our own, and dashboard-server plugins never enter an index. A list of integrations may exist only as optional plugins.

## Conventions

- **C4 levels.** C1 shows people, our system and external systems only. C2 shows deployable units inside our boundary, each with a technology label; deployment topology lives in a separate deployment view. C3 shows the components of one container. C4 shows code-level elements of one component. Every C4 diagram has a scope statement, an element table (id, name, technology, responsibility) and a table of relationships worth explaining.
- **Diagrams.** A figure is two lines: an image from `diagrams/png/<id>.png` and a "Figure" line that links its source under `diagrams/src/`. Standalone pages are under `diagrams/html/`, `c4-set.html` collects the set, and the render scripts are in `scripts/diagrams/` at the repository root. A figure is drawn from the element and relationship tables beside it, and every figure is checked as rendered. Containers, external systems and upstream processes are to carry role ids in every figure (`00` §8.1). That redraw is pending: 27 of the 72 figure sources still name an optional upstream, and their images, their standalone pages and `c4-set.html` were rendered before the second amendment of 2026-11-11, so they still show HolmesGPT as the investigator container and GoAlert as the on-call engine. Those 27 figures are stale against `00` §3.2 and §8.1, which win, until they are redrawn with role ids and checked as rendered (harness record §7.6). Mermaid C4 syntax is not used. A code-level classDiagram in `07` or the roadmap gantt in `08` may stay a fenced block until it is redrawn. A view that has no figure yet carries a `<!-- diagram: ... -->` placeholder next to its scope, element and relationship tables.
- **Vocabulary.** The set uses the terms of `00` §8.2: definition, registry, contribution and dispatcher; plugin and row; key class and binding; hook and audience; fiber and epoch; generation and distribution; community edition and enterprise edition; first community release; connector pack and connector scenario; publisher and index; `requires`, `serves` and support window; harness, run and pillar agent; loop row, external harness and departure; upstream, upstream plugin and stack bundle; upstream register and verdict; optional bundle. "Kernel" has two senses there: the module `sdk/kernel`, and the server binary with its `kernel*` containers. Phase tags follow `00` §7.2: P0 is the model core (S0, S1, S2a, S2b, S3, S4a, S5); P1 is the community release (S6a, S7, S8a, S8b), where S8a is the agent runtime and S8b the incident loop, whose exit is the first community release; P2 is two tracks (S9a beside S4b, S6b and S9b); P3 is S10. The enterprise edition is not planned, and no step has a date.
- **Owner decisions.** The community-first order of work is an owner decision of 2026-11-11 (`00` §2.4); it replaced W1, which is withdrawn. Decisions A, B and C of the second round of that date are owner decisions as well: the agent layer is built new with HolmesGPT as one optional harness plugin, GoAlert is one optional on-call provider plugin, and every licence is reviewed. W2 to W13 are owner decisions of 2026-11-11 too: they were written as working assumptions under ADR-033, and the owner confirmed each of them in words on that date. The labels W2 to W13 stay as the ids of those decisions and are cited as "(§2.4 Wn)"; changing one needs an ADR that edits `00`. W7 states the scope of the first-party harness of owner decision A at the first release. W11, the built-in on-call provider as the default, has the owner's yes, and its code still waits for a lawyer's answer on schedule patents (licence record Q3): if that answer is missing at S8b start, the first release ships providers only. What is still open after the confirmation is listed at the end of `00` §2.4. The list of P1 deliverables is pending the owner again (`00` §7.2), and the boundary between the two editions is open (ADR-049), so the set states it as a proposal.
- **Upstreams by role.** A decision sentence, a credential class, a saga stage or an acceptance script names a role, never an upstream; an upstream appears after "default:" or in a Technology or Rationale column, and acceptance scripts take a provider map (`00` §4.3, §7.1).
- **Licence statements.** Every licence statement in the set is engineering analysis, not legal advice. The verdict of an upstream is its entry in the upstream register, and an `unverified` entry blocks the release that first ships it (`00` §3.2).
- **Citations.** `00 §n` points into `00-decisions.md`; "plugin record §n" points into the plugin record; "community record §n" into the community record; "harness record §n" into the harness record; "licence record §n" into the licence record; research citations use `<file> §n` under [`../research/`](../research/). External URLs live in the research files, in community record §10 for the first amendment of 2026-11-11, and for licence facts in the source logs of the five licence audits and in licence record §9. Product requirements are stated in `00` on their technical merits and cited by section. The publication-rights gate remains **open** (`08` §5.1 L14): no repository content is disclosed outside the authorized audience until written confirmation of the rights to publish it under Apache-2.0 is recorded. Unpublished material stays out of Git, including history, and is not cited. Written owner confirmation of newly authored prompt and case content also precedes S8a.
- **Names.** Naming rules and the glossary are in `00-decisions.md` §8, including definition keys and hooks, plugin and row ids, the entity reference grammar (`<kind>:<id>`), the identity labels and the rule labels. No Grafana Labs mark appears in any product, plugin, row, bundle, profile or container name, and service-facing terms use generic nouns ("dashboard pools"). The same deny-list applies to every entry of the project index and of `contrib/`, whoever the publisher is; names for other vendors' systems follow community record §6.7. Outside the stated exceptions of `00` §4.3 (contracts such as the PostgreSQL dialect, and the base provider allow-list), the name of any other upstream appears in no definition, hook, schema, metric, container id or row of a bundle that a shipped profile lists; it appears at most once, as the lowercase suffix of the plugin that wraps it and of that plugin's own bundle, and only when its register entry allows it (`00` §8.1; licence record §7.5).
- **Trademark statement.** Every file that mentions a Grafana Labs mark ends with the policy's two-sentence statement, verbatim (`08` §5.2 T3).
- **Changing a decision.** Write an ADR in `docs/adr/` (process in `08` §3) and edit `00-decisions.md` in the same change.
